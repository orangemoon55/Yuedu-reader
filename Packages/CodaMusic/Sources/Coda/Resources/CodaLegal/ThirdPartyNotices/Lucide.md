- SVG: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/icons/letters.svg

The lyrics translation button uses the Lucide `languages` icon.

- Source: https://lucide.dev/icons/languages
- SVG: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/icons/languages.svg

- Source: https://lucide.dev/icons/heart
- SVG: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/icons/heart.svg

- Source: https://lucide.dev/icons/list-plus

- Source: https://lucide.dev/icons/star
